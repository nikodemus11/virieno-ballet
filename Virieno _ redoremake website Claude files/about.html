Our Story

Bringing Ballet to Nagaland

Virieno's Ballet was founded with a singular mission: to bring world-class classical ballet education to the Northeast Indian community. Starting as a home studio in 2022, we have grown into a beloved part of Nagaland's cultural landscape.


We teach the Russian Vaganova syllabus — one of the most respected classical ballet training methods in the world — in a warm, encouraging, and disciplined environment.
